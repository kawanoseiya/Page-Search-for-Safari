Page Search for Safari

Find text on a web page, the easy way.

Turn the extension on

  1. Open the Settings app
  2. Select Safari (inside Apps on iOS 18 and later)
  3. Tap Extensions
  4. Turn on Page Search
  5. Allow it to read the pages you search

How to use it

  1. Open a web page in Safari
  2. Tap the extensions button in the address bar and choose Page Search
  3. Type the keyword you are looking for
  4. Every match is highlighted on the page
  5. Use ‹ and › to move between matches
  6. Tap × to remove the highlights

Your keywords and the pages you read never leave your device. Page Search does not collect data and does not connect to any server.
